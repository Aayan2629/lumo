# Lumo Investor Mode: 100 things property investors look for

For the Microburbs Hackathon, Task 3 (buyer side). Items marked [MB] can likely come from the Microburbs API, [Open] from free open data, [Model] from our Task 4 rent model.

## 1. Price and value

1. Asking price vs recent comparable sales [MB]  
2. Estimated market value (automated valuation) [MB/Model]  
3. Price per square metre vs suburb [MB]  
4. Last sale price and date of this property [Open: NSW Valuer General]  
5. Capital growth of the suburb over 1, 5 and 10 years [MB]  
6. Price trend in the last 12 months (rising or falling) [MB]  
7. Land value from the Valuer General [Open]  
8. Land value as a share of total price [Open]  
9. Days on market vs suburb typical [MB]  
10. Price drops since first listed [MB]

## 2. Rental return

11. Estimated weekly rent [Model]  
12. Gross rental yield at asking price [Model]  
13. Net yield after strata, rates, insurance, management [Model]  
14. Suburb median yield for comparison [MB]  
15. Rent with vs without a car space [Model]  
16. Rental growth over recent years [MB]  
17. Vacancy rate in the suburb [MB]  
18. Days to lease for similar homes [MB]  
19. Rent history of this exact property [MB]  
20. Number of competing rentals nearby [MB]

## 3. Cash flow and costs

21. Weekly cash flow after mortgage repayments  
22. Stamp duty estimate (NSW)  
23. Strata levies per quarter  
24. Council rates  
25. Water rates  
26. Landlord insurance estimate  
27. Property management fees  
28. Deposit needed (10% and 20%)  
29. Lenders mortgage insurance estimate  
30. Depreciation potential (newer builds)

## 4. Supply and future development

31. Development applications within 300 m [Open: NSW Planning Portal]  
32. Number of new apartments approved in the suburb [Open]  
33. Large projects that could block views or add supply [Open]  
34. Zoning of the lot [Open: NSW Planning]  
35. Rezoning or upzoning potential [Open]  
36. Subdivision or granny flat potential [Open]  
37. Lot size and frontage [Open]  
38. Heritage listing or conservation area [Open]  
39. Planned infrastructure (metro lines, roads) [Open]  
40. Building pipeline vs population growth [Open]

## 5. Demand and demographics

41. Population growth [Open: ABS]  
42. Share of renters vs owners [Open: ABS]  
43. Median household income [Open: ABS]  
44. Rent as a share of local income (affordability) [Open: ABS]  
45. Age profile (students, young workers, families) [Open: ABS]  
46. Household size and people per bedroom [Open: ABS]  
47. Employment rate and main industries [Open: ABS]  
48. Distance to major job centres (CBD, hospitals, unis) [Open]  
49. Student demand (near universities) [Open]  
50. Search interest or listing views for the suburb [MB]

## 6. Location and transport

51. Walk time to nearest train or metro station [Open: OSM]  
52. Commute time to the CBD [Open: TfNSW]  
53. Bus and light rail access [Open]  
54. Walkability score [Open: OSM]  
55. Distance to supermarkets [Open: Overture]  
56. Cafes and restaurants nearby [MB]  
57. Parks and green space [Open: City of Sydney]  
58. Beach or waterfront access [MB]  
59. Street trees and shade [Open: City of Sydney]  
60. Parking availability on street

## 7. Risks

61. Flood zone or flood overlay [MB]  
62. Bushfire risk [Open: Digital Atlas]  
63. Crime rate vs national median [MB]  
64. Late-night venues and noise [MB]  
65. Main road, rail line or flight path noise [Open]  
66. Combustible cladding or building defects (apartments)  
67. Strata disputes or special levies  
68. Oversupply risk from nearby towers [Open]  
69. Investor-heavy building (many sales and rentals) [Open: Valuer General]  
70. Relisting frequency (tenants not staying) [MB]

## 8. The property itself

71. Property type (house, unit, townhouse)  
72. Bedrooms, bathrooms, car spaces  
73. Internal size in square metres  
74. Building age and condition  
75. Floor level and lift access  
76. Aspect and natural light (north-facing)  
77. Views and whether they are protected  
78. Outdoor space (balcony, courtyard)  
79. Storage and internal laundry  
80. Energy rating, solar, air conditioning

## 9. Strata and building

81. Number of units in the building  
82. Owner-occupier vs investor ratio  
83. Strata sinking fund balance  
84. Pet and short-stay (Airbnb) rules  
85. Building amenities (pool, gym, concierge)  
86. Recent strata works or upcoming repairs  
87. Builder and developer track record  
88. Building defect bonds or claims  
89. Body corporate manager quality  
90. Fees growth over time

## 10. Strategy and exit

91. Is it a growth play or a yield play?  
92. Renovation upside (value added vs cost)  
93. Resale demand from owner-occupiers  
94. Comparable homes for sale now (competition when selling) [MB]  
95. Suburb ranking for cash flow vs growth [MB: Suburb Finder]  
96. Tax: negative gearing and capital gains  
97. Land tax threshold  
98. Short-term rental potential and local rules  
99. Tenant profile it will attract  
100. Overall investor score: yield, growth, risk in one number
